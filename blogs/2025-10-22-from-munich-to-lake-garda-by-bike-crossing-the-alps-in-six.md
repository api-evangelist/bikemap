Crossing the Alps by bike — from Munich to Lake Garda — in just six days! Bikemap’s Web Lead, Sebastian, shares his unforgettable adventure through misty valleys, mountain passes, and sunlit vineyards. From the first pedal stroke in Munich to the final descent to the shimmering shores of Lake Garda, this journey is all about discovery, determination, and the pure joy of cycling.
