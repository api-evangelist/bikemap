From Naples to Rome by bike — through medieval hilltop towns, ancient Roman roads, and one very fast sausage dog — in just four days! Sarah Sulollari and her partner Robert, Bikemap's Community Lead, share their unforgettable adventure through the heart of Italy. From the chaotic streets of Naples in the shadow of Mount Vesuvius to the final cobblestones of the legendary Via Appia, this journey is all about unexpected moments, hard-earned climbs, and the pure joy of cycling somewhere truly special.
